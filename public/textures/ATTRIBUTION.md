# Planet texture attribution

The following texture maps are derived from the free Solar System Scope texture library and are used under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International license:

- `4k_mercury.jpg`
- `2k_venus_atmosphere.jpg`
- `4k_mars.jpg`
- `4k_jupiter.jpg`
- `4k_saturn.jpg`
- `2k_saturn_ring_alpha.png`
- `2k_uranus.jpg`
- `2k_neptune.jpg`

Source: [Solar System Scope Textures](https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/)

License: [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)

The Mercury and Mars source maps were resized to 4096 × 2048 for reliable WebGL rendering. No other visual modifications were made to the downloaded source maps.
